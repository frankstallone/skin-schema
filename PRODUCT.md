# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Skin Schema serves beauty-brand decision makers: founders managing their own marketing, in-house marketers, and creative agency producers. They need a trusted creative partner who can produce distinctive, campaign-quality skincare, makeup, and wellness content without adding disorder or unnecessary friction to their workflow.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Skin Schema's work, experience, and collaborative approach so prospective clients want to work with the creator and reach out about a brief. Success means visitors recognize the quality of the work, trust the process, and see the content as a durable investment they can reuse month after month rather than as a short-lived social deliverable.

## Positioning

Skin Schema delivers polished, distinctive beauty content through an organized, customizable collaboration, giving brands campaign-quality assets they can reuse month after month.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: reach out to collaborate.
- Secondary fallback: save Skin Schema's contact details for a future brief.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: “I want to work with this person.”
- Belief ladder: the work is genuinely high quality; Skin Schema understands beauty products and brand standards; the collaboration will be organized and customizable; the resulting content will remain useful and capable of creating value well beyond one campaign.
- Proof on hand: work across 20+ brands and approximately 40 campaigns; portfolio photography and video; approved client logos for Armani Beauty, Valentino, Naturopathica, Queen Musia, and Saya. Testimonials are not yet available and should be requested from future clients. Client-side performance and revenue metrics are not currently visible to Skin Schema and should not be claimed without evidence.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, beautiful, and organized. The brand should make prospective clients feel excited, confident, inspired, and determined to work with Skin Schema. Its tone should convey polish and experience while remaining warm and approachable.

Directional references include Queen Musia for product photography and art direction; Biologique Recherche for luxury positioning, minimalist product presentation, customized solutions, and an apothecary sensibility; Naturopathica and Romilly Wilde for their overall direction; and creator ilievalisa for color grading, Instagram presentation, content quality, and self-positioning.

## Anti-references

Skin Schema should never feel discount-oriented, cold, unapproachable, cheap, or messy. It should avoid generic luxury signals that obscure the creator's distinctive work or make the collaboration feel impersonal.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with work that makes the decision visceral: visitors should see the quality before they are asked to believe claims about it.
2. Make polish feel operational as well as visual: clear structure and thoughtful details should demonstrate an organized collaboration.
3. Present customization as expertise, showing that content is shaped around each brand and product rather than produced from a formula.
4. Frame content as a durable brand asset with a useful life beyond a single post or campaign.
5. Pair luxury-level craft with human warmth so high standards never become cold or inaccessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable color contrast, descriptive alternative text and media labels, and reduced-motion preferences. Keep content understandable and interactions usable across responsive web layouts.
